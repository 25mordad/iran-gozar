# Rules-of-the-game pact (draft)

> **In plain words**
>
> This is a short text that groups and people with very different views could agree on today.
> It says nothing about what Iran's future should look like; it only says how we compete and disagree about that future.
> Signers promise not to eliminate any rival, to accept the result of a free vote, and to defend a rival whose rights are violated.
> An AI wrote this text, and it remains only a draft until groups from different camps have criticized and rewritten it.

## Why this pact?

In Iran's modern history, the loser in politics has often lost everything — and so every group tries to eliminate its rival before the rival gains power. The best time to agree on rules is when no one knows who will be stronger tomorrow: **now, before day zero.** The full argument is in the Persian document [Accepting the rules of the game](topics/rules-of-the-game.md).

The pact sets **rules**, not **outcomes**. A constitutional monarchist and a republican, a federalist and a centralist, a religious and a secular Iranian can all sign it while keeping their own views.

## Text of the pact

**Version 0.1, a draft for criticism**

We, the signers, holding different views about Iran's future, agree on these rules and declare them publicly:

1. **Power only from a free vote.** None of us claims a right to govern except through free, secret, universal and periodic elections under independent oversight.
2. **No elimination.** No person or group is deprived of political or civil rights because of their beliefs, identity, religion or view on the form of government. Punishment is only for individual crimes, and only by a fair court.
3. **No weapons.** We do not pursue political disagreements through weapons, threats, assassination or intimidation, and we support no group that does.
4. **Accepting the result.** We accept the result of a free vote certified by independent observers, even if we lose, and we pursue objections only through legal channels and the courts.
5. **Losing is not the end.** If we win, we guarantee that the losers remain free, can organize and have an equal chance in the next election. No election is the last election.
6. **Defending the rival.** If the rights of any signer of this pact — even our rival — are violated, we will defend them publicly.
7. **Balanced arbitration.** The referees and supervisors of the transition, such as the election commission and the interim constitutional court, are chosen by a method in which all sides have a share, not by the decision of one side.
8. **Rules before outcomes.** On the form of government, decentralization, religion and state and other contested questions, we agree on how the decision will be made before competing over the outcome, and we accept the outcome of that method.
9. **No foreign force.** We do not call for foreign military intervention to defeat a domestic rival, we take neither weapons nor orders from any foreign government, and we disclose our funding.
10. **Language without hatred.** We do not call rivals enemies, traitors or foreigners; we do not allow our supporters to harass or threaten rivals, and if they do, we publicly distance ourselves from it.

## What this pact is not

- **Not a coalition.** Signers remain rivals and compete for their own views.
- **Not a political program.** It takes no position on any policy, from the economy to the form of government.
- **Not about leadership.** It makes no person or group the leader, representative or spokesperson of others.
- **Not an endorsement of the rest of this plan.** Signing it does not mean accepting any of Gozar's other proposals.
- **Not an amnesty.** It concerns political competition and does not replace fair justice for past crimes (see [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md), Persian).

## Who can sign, and how

- **Groups, parties, networks and civil-society organizations:** any group that renounces violence as a path to power, whatever its views.
- **Signed on the signer's own channel:** a valid signature is the publication of the pact (or of a rewritten version of it) on the group's own website or official channel. This page only links to those publications. Anyone can verify a signature without trusting this project or any other intermediary.
- **People inside Iran:** no public signature is asked of anyone living in Iran. Your safety comes first. If you want to comment, first read the [safe contribution guide](project/security.md).
- **This project collects no list of people's names.**

## How this text changes

- An AI wrote this draft, and that is its main weakness. The proposal is that a small group from different camps rewrites the final text, and that each clause becomes final only when none of them has a "fundamental objection" to it.
- Every version is numbered, and the changes in each version are published with reasons.
- Any signer who wishes to leave the pact announces it publicly, with reasons.
- Send criticism and proposed changes through the [critique form](https://github.com/25mordad/iran-gozar/issues/new?template=critique.yml&document=docs/rules-pact.md).
