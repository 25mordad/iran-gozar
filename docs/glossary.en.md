# Glossary

> **In plain words**
>
> This page explains, in plain language, the key words used in the transition documents.
> Some are names of bodies this plan proposes for the transition, such as the "Interim Transitional Council".
> Others are common terms in politics, law and economics, such as "referendum" or "transitional justice".
> Some are names of institutions and practices in Iran today, such as "approbatory supervision" or "gozinesh".
> Each entry gives the Persian term and a link to the document that explains it further.

## About this glossary

- All documents in this project use the same words for the core concepts (see [decisions](decisions.md)). If a document uses a term that does not match this page, please [report it](participate.md).
- Entries are in English alphabetical order. The Persian version of this page follows the Persian alphabet.
- Definitions are short and neutral and pass no judgment on any person or group. The full explanation and arguments are in the linked document.
- Names of bodies proposed by this plan are proposals, not decisions; the final decision belongs to elected institutions and a free vote of the people.
- Any specific claim without a firm source is marked [citation needed].

## A

### Additional Protocol

*پروتکل الحاقی* — A voluntary agreement that extends the IAEA's inspection powers beyond ordinary safeguards. Iran signed it in 2003, but parliament never ratified it [citation needed]. See [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### Approbatory supervision

*نظارت استصوابی* — The Guardian Council's interpretation of its duty to supervise elections (Article 99), which lets it approve or reject candidates before the vote. Critics see it as incompatible with free and competitive elections, and this plan replaces it with an independent electoral commission. See [Elections](institutions/elections.md), [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

### Article 44 of the constitution

*اصل ۴۴ قانون اساسی* — The article that bases Iran's economy on three sectors: state, cooperative and private. After its general policies were issued in the 2000s, many state companies were divested, but a large share went to non-governmental public and quasi-state bodies [citation needed]. See [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md).

### Assembly of Experts

*مجلس خبرگان رهبری* — An assembly of Islamic jurists elected by popular vote after the Guardian Council approves the candidates. Under Article 107 it chooses the Leader, and under Article 111 it decides on dismissing the Leader if he becomes unable to perform his duties. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

### Asset declaration

*اعلام دارایی* — Requiring public officials to declare their and their close family's assets, income and interests, with public disclosure and verification. Under this plan, transitional officials do so first. See [Anti-corruption](topics/anti-corruption.md), [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

## B

### Basij

*بسیج* — An organization founded in 1979 that later became one of the IRGC's forces [citation needed: exact dates], with a network of bases in neighborhoods, schools, universities and workplaces. This plan proposes ending its paramilitary and street activity, continuing members' pay until a final plan is announced, and converting its relief activities. See [IRGC and Basij](institutions/irgc-basij.md).

### Beneficial owner

*مالک واقعی (مالک ذی‌نفع)* — The natural person who ultimately owns or controls a company or benefits from it, even if their name is not on official records. A public beneficial ownership register is a key tool against money laundering and corruption. See [Anti-corruption](topics/anti-corruption.md), [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md).

### Bonyad (foundation)

*بنیاد* — Institutions such as the Mostazafan Foundation, some formed after the revolution from confiscated property, that today combine welfare or charity work with extensive business activity without being accountable to elected bodies [citation needed]. This plan proposes keeping their welfare functions, placing their assets under trustee management and leaving final decisions to elected bodies. See [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md).

## C

### Cash transfer

*یارانه‌ی نقدی* — Paying money directly to households instead of keeping prices artificially low. In Iran it has been paid since 2010 under the subsidy-targeting law [citation needed]. See [Public finance](institutions/public-finance.md), [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Chain of custody

*زنجیره‌ی نگهداری شواهد* — A continuous record of who held each document or item of evidence, and where, from discovery to court, so that it remains admissible. See [Intelligence](institutions/intelligence.md), [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Chamber of provinces

*مجلس استان‌ها* — A second chamber representing the provinces alongside a chamber representing the population. The parliament document proposes it only if decentralization is real, and with limited powers. See [Parliament](institutions/parliament.md), [Ethnic rights and decentralization](topics/ethnic-rights-decentralization.md).

### City and village councils

*شوراهای شهر و روستا* — Councils that, under Article 100 of the constitution, are elected to run local affairs and choose mayors [citation needed]. See [Municipalities](institutions/municipalities.md).

### Civilian control of the armed forces

*کنترل غیرنظامی بر نیروهای مسلح* — The principle that the armed forces take orders from accountable civilian officials, that elected representatives oversee their budget and activities, and that they stay out of politics and business. See [Army](institutions/army.md), [Security and civil war prevention](topics/security-and-civil-war-prevention.md).

### Civilian defense minister

*وزیر دفاع غیرنظامی* — A civilian minister with public management experience and knowledge of defense, appointed by the interim government; the army commander reports through this minister to the interim government and council. See [Army](institutions/army.md).

### Collective punishment

*مجازات جمعی* — Punishing a group, family, ethnic community or an institution's staff for the acts of some individuals. Banning it is one of this plan's red lines; responsibility is only individual. See [Manifesto](manifesto.md), [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Command responsibility

*مسئولیت فرماندهی* — A legal principle under which a commander who knew of crimes by subordinates and, despite being able to, did not prevent them is held responsible. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Conditional amnesty

*عفو مشروط* — Individual pardon for less serious wrongdoing linked to repression, only in exchange for full truth, return of illicit gains and public acceptance of responsibility. There is no blanket amnesty, and crimes against humanity, torture, extrajudicial killing, enforced disappearance, sexual violence and grand corruption are never eligible. Decisions are made by an independent conditional amnesty committee that includes judges, and both applicants and victims can appeal. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Conflict of interest

*تعارض منافع* — A situation in which an official's personal, family or financial interests clash with their public duty. The rule is to declare it and stay out of the related decision. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md), [Diaspora](topics/diaspora.md).

### Conscription

*نظام وظیفه* — Compulsory military service for men in Iran. In phase two this plan suspends fines and travel bans for draft evaders and leaves decisions on its future, including a professional army or alternative civilian service, to the elected parliament. See [Army](institutions/army.md), [Diaspora](topics/diaspora.md).

### Constituent assembly

*مجلس مؤسسان* — An assembly elected between month six and month ten at the latest to write the new constitution within at most 12 months. The interim council's powers end when it first meets. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Constitutional court

*دادگاه قانون اساسی* — A court that reviews whether laws and government decisions comply with the constitution. During the transition, an interim constitutional court or independent legal panel checks that the draft constitution complies with the core principles of the transitional charter. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md), [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Constitutional monarchy

*پادشاهی مشروطه* — A system in which the head of state is a hereditary monarch whose powers are limited by the constitution, and the country is run by a government accountable to an elected parliament. The choice between this form and a republic belongs to a free vote of the people. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Constructive vote of no confidence

*رأی عدم اعتماد سازنده* — A rule under which parliament can remove the government only by electing a replacement at the same time, as in the German and Spanish constitutions. See [Parliament](institutions/parliament.md), [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Correspondent banking

*بانکداری کارگزاری* — An arrangement in which one bank provides payment and transfer services for a bank in another country. Without it, connecting a country to the global financial system is very difficult. See [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md), [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md).

### Court of Administrative Justice

*دیوان عدالت اداری* — A court that, under Article 173, hears citizens' complaints against government officials, agencies and regulations. Under this plan, it hears appeals against vetting decisions on public employees. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md), [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

### Crimes against humanity

*جنایت علیه بشریت* — Acts such as murder, torture, enforced disappearance or sexual violence committed as part of a widespread or systematic attack on a civilian population; the best-known definition is in the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

## D

### Day zero

*روز صفر* — The moment when the current power structure can no longer, or will no longer, govern and an interim transitional authority takes responsibility. Most of the plan's timelines are counted from this moment; in some scenarios the clock starts differently. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Regime change scenarios](topics/regime-change-scenarios.md).

### Decentralization

*تمرکززدایی* — Transferring part of the powers, budget and administration from the central government to provinces, cities and villages and their elected bodies, while the country's sovereignty remains unified. See [Ethnic rights and decentralization](topics/ethnic-rights-decentralization.md), [Governorates](institutions/governorates.md).

### Diaspora

*دیاسپورا* — Iranians living abroad and their children. This plan's principle for their involvement is: supporters, not rulers. See [Diaspora](topics/diaspora.md).

### Disarmament, demobilization and reintegration (DDR)

*خلع سلاح، انحلال ساختار نظامی و بازگشت به زندگی مدنی (DDR)* — A program in which members of armed or paramilitary groups hand in their weapons, their military structures are dissolved, and they return to civilian life with vocational training, transitional allowances and social support. It is designed on the UN's integrated standards (IDDRS) and is not an amnesty. See [Security and civil war prevention](topics/security-and-civil-war-prevention.md), [IRGC and Basij](institutions/irgc-basij.md).

### Dual citizenship

*تابعیت دوگانه* — Holding Iranian and another nationality at the same time. This plan proposes accepting it, with restrictions for a small number of sensitive posts; the final decision belongs to the new constitution. See [Diaspora](topics/diaspora.md).

## E

### Emergency economic council

*شورای اضطراری اقتصادی* — A council within the interim government made up of economic ministers and the central bank governor, with worker and employer representatives and independent economists as observers and advisers. Its decisions are published within 24 hours. See [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Endowments (awqaf)

*اوقاف* — A waqf is the permanent dedication of an asset to a religious or charitable purpose; awqaf are these assets and the bodies that manage them. This plan proposes transparent audits of endowments, respect for donors' intentions and a new law governing them. See [Religious institutions](institutions/religious-institutions.md), [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md).

### Enforced disappearance

*ناپدیدسازی قهری* — The arrest or abduction of a person by state agents or with their support, followed by denial of the detention or concealment of the person's fate and whereabouts. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Enrichment

*غنی‌سازی* — Increasing the share of the uranium-235 isotope in uranium. Low enrichment is used for power reactor fuel, while very high enrichment can be used for weapons. This plan leaves the final decision on its future to elected bodies. See [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### Evidence and archives preservation board

*هیئت حفظ شواهد و اسناد* — A board under independent judges and lawyers, set up in the first 30 days, to protect archives and evidence of human rights violations from destruction, looting and misuse. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Exchange-rate unification

*یکسان‌سازی نرخ ارز (تک‌نرخی شدن ارز)* — Ending multiple exchange rates and moving to a single rate. This plan proposes doing it on a published timetable together with cash support for low-income households. See [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md), [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Expediency Discernment Council

*مجمع تشخیص مصلحت نظام* — A body whose members are appointed by the Leader; under Article 112 it resolves disputes between parliament and the Guardian Council and advises the Leader on the system's general policies. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

### Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (EITI)

*ابتکار شفافیت صنایع استخراجی (EITI)* — An international standard that brings government, companies and civil society together in a joint group to publish and verify oil, gas and mining revenues and payments. Joining it is one option in this plan. See [Oil and energy](institutions/oil-energy.md).

## F

### Fact-checking

*راستی‌آزمایی* — Checking whether a report, claim, photo or video is accurate against independent sources, before publication or in response to rumors. See [Public communication](topics/public-communication.md), [Media](institutions/media.md).

### Fair trial

*دادرسی عادلانه* — The set of guarantees, such as an independent and impartial court, the presumption of innocence, access to a lawyer, public hearings and the right of appeal, without which no verdict is legitimate. See [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md), [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Family information center

*مرکز اطلاعات خانواده‌ها* — A phone line and walk-in desk in every province where families can ask where their relatives are detained. The police share their lists of detainees with it. See [Prisons](institutions/prisons.md), [Police](institutions/police.md).

### Federalism

*فدرالیسم* — A system in which the constitution divides power between the central government and regional units, each with guaranteed powers. In the Iranian debate both territorial and ethnic versions are proposed, and the decision belongs to elected bodies and voters. See [Ethnic rights and decentralization](topics/ethnic-rights-decentralization.md).

### Financial Action Task Force (FATF)

*گروه ویژه‌ی اقدام مالی (FATF)* — An intergovernmental body that sets global standards against money laundering (hiding the origin of criminal money) and terrorist financing and assesses how countries apply them. Iran is on the list of jurisdictions subject to a call for countermeasures [citation needed]. See [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md), [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md).

### Fiscal equalization

*هم‌ترازسازی مالی* — Transferring resources from the central government to provinces or cities according to a transparent formula, so that poorer areas can provide comparable basic services. See [Governorates](institutions/governorates.md), [Public finance](institutions/public-finance.md).

## G

### Gozinesh (ideological screening)

*گزینش* — Ideological and political screening of applicants for public-sector jobs and, in some cases, university admission and faculty hiring, under the "Gozinesh law" [citation needed: text of the law], covering religion and loyalty to the system and to velayat-e faqih. This plan proposes halting it within 30 days and repealing it by month six. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md), [Universities](institutions/universities.md).

### Guarantees of non-recurrence

*تضمین عدم تکرار* — Reforming the institutions and laws that made past violations possible, so that they become hard or impossible to repeat; one of the four pillars of transitional justice. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Guardian Council

*شورای نگهبان* — A twelve-member council of six jurists of Islamic law appointed by the Leader and six lawyers nominated by the head of the judiciary and approved by parliament (Article 91). It vets parliament's laws against Islamic law and the constitution and supervises elections. Under this plan, its powers are suspended by the interim council's first decree. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md), [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### Guidance patrol

*گشت ارشاد* — Police patrols that have enforced compulsory hijab and dress rules in public places [citation needed: current status and name]. This plan proposes dissolving them and similar bodies within the first 30 days. See [Police](institutions/police.md), [Women's rights](topics/women-rights.md).

## H

### Hawza (seminary)

*حوزه‌ی علمیه* — Traditional schools of Shia religious learning, including in Qom. This plan supports the seminaries' self-governance and their separation from the state. See [Religious institutions](institutions/religious-institutions.md).

### Herasat (protection units)

*حراست* — Units inside government agencies that, besides physical and information security, have also played a role in monitoring employees' political activity [citation needed]. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

## I

### Independent anti-corruption agency

*نهاد مستقل مبارزه با فساد* — An agency whose members are chosen through a transparent process approved by the national advisory assembly, with fixed terms and a guaranteed budget, responsible for prevention, financial investigation and checking asset declarations. See [Anti-corruption](topics/anti-corruption.md).

### Independent central bank

*بانک مرکزی مستقل* — A central bank whose governor has a fixed term and which the government cannot force to print money for current spending. Its main goals are controlling inflation and keeping the banking system stable. See [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md).

### Independent civil service commission

*کمیسیون مستقل خدمات کشوری* — A non-partisan body that runs recruitment exams, oversees senior appointments through open competition and handles employment complaints, replacing ideological screening. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

### Independent electoral commission

*کمیسیون مستقل انتخابات* — A non-partisan body with its own budget, set up in phase three, that runs elections and referendums in place of the interior ministry's election headquarters and approbatory supervision. See [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### Independent police complaints commission

*کمیسیون مستقل رسیدگی به شکایات از پلیس* — A body outside the police and armed forces with powers to investigate, access records and refer cases to prosecutors. Every death or serious injury involving the police goes to it automatically. See [Police](institutions/police.md).

### Individual vetting

*بازبینی فردی* — A review of each person's own conduct and record, not their membership in an institution, with written allegations, the right to a defense and a lawyer, and the right to appeal, so that people involved in serious violations do not remain in positions of public trust. It is not a punishment, and it replaces collective purges. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md), [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

### Interim high judicial council

*شورای موقت عالی قضایی* — A council of 9 to 11 members that takes over management of the judiciary between months three and six under the transitional charter and begins vetting judges. Until it forms, an interim caretaker board of three to five senior judges runs the judiciary's administration. See [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

### Interim intelligence oversight committee

*کمیته‌ی موقت نظارت بر نهادهای اطلاعاتی* — A committee of national advisory assembly members, a judge and human rights experts that oversees the intelligence agencies until a parliamentary committee is formed. See [Intelligence](institutions/intelligence.md).

### Interim legal board

*هیئت موقت حقوقی* — A small group of independent lawyers and judges that, until the transitional charter is adopted, reviews the interim council's decrees against its declared principles and fundamental rights and publishes its opinions. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

### Interim local councils

*شوراهای محلی موقت* — City and village councils elected at the same time as the constituent assembly, in provinces where the independent electoral commission certifies readiness. See [Municipalities](institutions/municipalities.md), [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Interim technocratic government

*دولت موقت کاردان* — A cabinet of experienced and respected managers and experts, from inside and outside today's institutions, that runs the country day to day during the interim period. Selection is based on competence, integrity and commitment to the transitional charter, not group loyalty. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

### Interim transitional authority

*مرجع موقت گذار* — The general name for the body that governs from day zero until the constituent assembly first meets. This plan proposes an interim transitional council working with an interim technocratic government. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Interim Transitional Council

*شورای موقت گذار* — A small, diverse council of 9 to 15 members that holds political responsibility during the interim period. It lasts at most 12 months, and its members do not run in the constituent assembly election or the first election for the highest executive office. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)

*آژانس بین‌المللی انرژی اتمی* — An international organization in the UN family that promotes the peaceful use of nuclear energy and monitors countries' nuclear material and facilities through inspections and safeguards. See [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### International humanitarian law

*حقوق بین‌الملل بشردوستانه* — Rules, above all the Geneva Conventions, that limit conduct in armed conflict and protect civilians, the wounded, prisoners and medical staff. See [Army](institutions/army.md), [Health](institutions/health.md).

### IRIB (state broadcaster)

*صداوسیما* — Iran's state radio and television organization, whose head is appointed by the Leader under Article 175 and which holds a monopoly on broadcasting [citation needed]. This plan proposes turning it into independent public service media. See [Media](institutions/media.md).

### Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)

*سپاه پاسداران انقلاب اسلامی* — An armed force that, under Article 150 of the constitution, exists alongside the army to guard the revolution and its achievements, and also plays roles in the economy and politics. This plan proposes not overnight dissolution but deciding each part's future by its function, with gradual integration and individual vetting. See [IRGC and Basij](institutions/irgc-basij.md).

### Istanbul and Minnesota protocols

*پروتکل‌های استانبول و مینه‌سوتا* — Two UN manuals for forensic doctors and lawyers: the Istanbul Protocol on effective investigation and documentation of torture, and the Minnesota Protocol on the investigation of potentially unlawful death. See [Prisons](institutions/prisons.md), [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

## J

### JCPOA (Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action)

*برجام* — The 2015 nuclear agreement between Iran and the P5+1 that placed limits on Iran's nuclear program in exchange for sanctions relief. The United States withdrew in May 2018. See [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md), [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### Judicial independence

*استقلال قضایی* — Judges' freedom from pressure by the government, parties, armed forces and other bodies when deciding cases, secured by transparent appointment, security of tenure and an independent budget. See [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

## K

### Khosulati (nominally privatized firms)

*خصولتی* — A colloquial word for companies and banks that were privatized on paper but whose managers are still chosen by public or quasi-state bodies, and which in many cases escape proper oversight. See [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md), [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md).

## L

### Lustration

*غربال* — In the transition literature, barring people from public office because of collaboration with the former regime; well-known examples are laws in some Central and Eastern European countries after 1989. Collective lustration is based on membership; this plan accepts only its individual form, individual vetting. See [Lessons from other transitions](topics/lessons-from-transitions.md), [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

## M

### Majoritarian electoral system

*نظام انتخاباتی اکثریتی* — A system in which the candidate or candidates with the most votes in each district win; simple and familiar, but many votes end up wasted. See [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### Mixed electoral system

*نظام انتخاباتی مختلط* — A system that fills some seats by majority vote in districts and others proportionally from party lists. See [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### Moncloa Pacts

*پیمان‌های مونکلوا* — The 1977 agreements between the government and the main parties in Spain during the post-Franco transition, which combined curbing inflation with tax reform, expanded social security and legal reforms [citation needed: details of the text]. See [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md), [Lessons from other transitions](topics/lessons-from-transitions.md).

### Mutual legal assistance

*معاضدت قضایی* — Formal cooperation between countries' judicial authorities to gather evidence, trace and freeze assets and enforce judgments. See [Anti-corruption](topics/anti-corruption.md), [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

## N

### National Advisory Assembly

*مجمع مشورتی ملی* — A broad assembly of representatives of professions, universities, provinces, minorities, workers, women and Iranians abroad, with the right to question the interim council and government in public. Some key appointments, such as members of the truth commission and the independent electoral commission, require its approval. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### National Conference

*کنفرانس ملی* — A broad gathering in the first weeks that confirms or amends the membership of the interim transitional council and later adopts the transitional charter after public consultation. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### National Information Network

*شبکه‌ی ملی اطلاعات* — A project officially described as domestic infrastructure, data centers and local internet services [citation needed]. Critics see it as a tool that makes it easier to cut the connection to the global internet while keeping domestic services running. See [Telecom and internet](institutions/telecom-internet.md).

### National reconciliation

*آشتی ملی* — A long-term process of rebuilding trust between groups that were on opposite sides of repression or conflict. In this plan, reconciliation rests on truth and justice, not forgetting. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

## O

### Open data

*داده‌ی باز* — Publishing public data, such as budgets, contracts and decrees, in a free, searchable and reusable format for everyone. See [Public finance](institutions/public-finance.md), [Public communication](topics/public-communication.md).

## P

### Parliamentary system

*نظام پارلمانی* — A system in which the government emerges from parliament and is accountable to it; if parliament loses confidence in the government, the government goes. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md), [Parliament](institutions/parliament.md).

### Preferential exchange rate

*ارز ترجیحی* — Foreign currency that the government sells below the market rate to importers of certain goods. This plan's documents see multiple exchange rates as a source of rent-seeking and corruption. See [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md), [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Presidential system

*نظام ریاستی* — A system in which the head of government is elected separately and directly, serves a fixed term and does not depend on parliament's confidence. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md), [Parliament](institutions/parliament.md).

### Principle of continuity of service

*اصل ادامه‌ی خدمت* — The principle that all public employees stay in their jobs and keep being paid, and that anyone can be removed only through a defined, individual process open to appeal. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md).

### Property claims commission

*کمیسیون رسیدگی به دعاوی اموال* — An independent commission, established by a law passed by an elected body, to handle claims over confiscated property for all citizens, through restitution or compensation and without evicting occupants without due process. See [Diaspora](topics/diaspora.md).

### Proportional representation

*نظام انتخاباتی تناسبی* — A system in which seats are shared out in proportion to each list's or party's votes, so that groups are represented according to their support. This plan proposes it for the constituent assembly election. See [Elections](institutions/elections.md).

### Provincial advisory council

*شورای مشورتی استانی* — A council of local representatives in each province, formed by day 30, that advises on provincial administration and oversees the provincial budget until provincial councils are elected. See [Governorates](institutions/governorates.md), [Ethnic rights and decentralization](topics/ethnic-rights-decentralization.md).

### Provincial crisis headquarters

*ستاد بحران استان* — A body in each province, chaired by the governor or acting governor, bringing together police, health, water, power, gas, fuel, bread, Red Crescent and major-city mayors to manage the first days. See [Governorates](institutions/governorates.md), [Essential services](topics/essential-services.md).

### Provincial security council

*شورای تأمین استان* — A council in each province chaired by the governor with police, military, intelligence and judicial officials [citation needed: legal composition], which decides on handling protests and coordinating the forces, and whose decisions are not public. See [Governorates](institutions/governorates.md).

### Public service media

*رسانه‌ی عمومی* — Media funded from public money but independent of the government and parties, and accountable to all citizens rather than to those in power. See [Media](institutions/media.md).

## Q

### Quasi-state economic conglomerates

*نهادهای اقتصادی شبه‌دولتی* — A mixed group of bonyads, companies linked to the armed forces and nominally privatized firms that are neither fully state-owned nor truly private: they enjoy state privileges but are not accountable to elected bodies. See [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md).

## R

### Rapid review panels

*هیئت‌های بازبینی فوری* — Panels of respected judges, criminal defense lawyers and human rights documenters who review prisoners' files from the first days so that political prisoners and prisoners of conscience are released. See [Prisons](institutions/prisons.md).

### Red lines

*خطوط قرمز* — Commitments in this project's manifesto that are never broken, including no extra-legal killings, a moratorium on executions during the transition, no torture or collective punishment, no partition and no open-ended transition. See [Manifesto](manifesto.md).

### Referendum

*همه‌پرسی* — A direct vote by all citizens on a question or a text, such as a constitution or the form of government. Under this plan, no referendum on the form of government is held in the first six months, to allow free debate. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Reparations

*جبران خسارت* — Redress for victims in several forms: restitution (such as return of property or reinstatement), financial compensation, medical and psychological rehabilitation, and symbolic measures such as an official apology or disclosure of burial sites. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Reparations fund

*صندوق جبران خسارت* — A fund with a legal basis and an independent board that includes victims' representatives, delivering compensation, rehabilitation and other forms of reparation to victims of past violations. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Republic

*جمهوری* — A system in which the head of state is chosen by the people or their representatives for a limited term, not by inheritance. The choice between this form and a constitutional monarchy belongs to a free vote of the people. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Restorative justice

*عدالت ترمیمی* — An approach that, rather than focusing only on punishment, emphasizes repairing harm, dialogue between victim and perpetrator, and rebuilding social ties. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md), [Civil liberties](topics/civil-liberties.md).

### Revolutionary courts

*دادگاه انقلاب* — Courts that were meant to be temporary after 1979, are not named in the constitution, and hear security, political and major drug-trafficking cases [citation needed]. This plan proposes abolishing them and transferring their cases to the ordinary courts. See [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

## S

### Safeguards

*پادمان* — The IAEA's system of inspections and accounting to verify that a country's nuclear material is not diverted from peaceful uses to weapons. See [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### Secondary sanctions

*تحریم‌های ثانویه* — Sanctions that the sanctioning country applies not only to the main target but also to companies and banks in other countries that do business with it. See [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md).

### Security sector reform

*اصلاح بخش امنیت* — Rebuilding the army, police, intelligence agencies and related bodies so that they are professional, accountable, under civilian command and bound by law and human rights. See [Security and civil war prevention](topics/security-and-civil-war-prevention.md), [Army](institutions/army.md).

### Semi-presidential system

*نظام نیمه‌ریاستی* — A system with a popularly elected president alongside a prime minister accountable to parliament, as in France's Fifth Republic. See [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Snapback

*اسنپ‌بک* — The automatic sanctions-restoration mechanism in UN Security Council Resolution 2231 (2015), which let JCPOA participants restore earlier UN sanctions in a way that a single permanent member's veto could not block. In 2025 three European countries triggered it, and Iran and some other states rejected the legitimacy of the process [citation needed]. See [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md).

### Special chambers for serious crimes

*شعبه‌های ویژه‌ی رسیدگی به جنایات جدی* — Chambers within the reformed courts, staffed by judges who have passed individual vetting, that try the serious-crime cases brought by the special prosecutor. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md), [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

### Special Clerical Court

*دادگاه ویژه‌ی روحانیت* — A court that tries clerics, is not provided for in the constitution and operates directly under the Leader, outside the judiciary [citation needed]. This plan proposes abolishing it. See [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md), [Religious freedom](topics/religious-freedom.md).

### Special prosecutor's office

*دادسرای ویژه* — An independent prosecution service for investigating and prosecuting past serious crimes, led by a prosecutor appointed for a fixed term through a transparent process with the approval of the national advisory assembly, and with a protected budget. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md), [Judiciary](institutions/judiciary.md).

### Supreme Audit Court

*دیوان محاسبات کشور* — A body under parliament that, under Articles 54 and 55, audits government agencies' accounts to ensure spending stays within the approved budget. See [Public finance](institutions/public-finance.md), [Parliament](institutions/parliament.md).

### Supreme Council of Cyberspace

*شورای عالی فضای مجازی* — A council created by the Leader's decree, under which major internet policies, including filtering and the National Information Network, have been set [citation needed]. This plan proposes suspending and then dissolving it. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md), [Telecom and internet](institutions/telecom-internet.md).

### Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution

*شورای عالی انقلاب فرهنگی* — A council not provided for in the constitution, whose decisions on universities, screening of faculty and students, and major education policy documents have in practice been enforced like laws [citation needed]. This plan proposes suspending it on day zero and then dissolving it. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md), [Universities](institutions/universities.md).

### Supreme National Security Council

*شورای عالی امنیت ملی* — A body that, under Article 176, sets defense and security policy within the general policies determined by the Leader; its decisions take effect after the Leader approves them. Under this plan, the Transitional National Security Council replaces it. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

## T

### Temporary capital controls

*کنترل موقت سرمایه* — Temporary, public and time-limited restrictions on sending foreign currency abroad and on very large withdrawals, to prevent capital flight and bank runs during a crisis. Everyday payments and imports of medicine and food are exempt. See [Central bank](institutions/central-bank.md), [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Thirty-month cap

*سقف ۳۰ ماهه‌ی گذار* — The maximum length of the whole transition, from day zero to the handover of power to institutions elected under the new constitution. No unelected body can extend it. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Transitional charter

*منشور گذار* — A short interim constitution adopted by month six. It sets out fundamental rights, red lines, the structure of interim bodies, the timetable, and core principles that the future constitution may not violate. See [Transition phases](transition-phases.md), [Constitution](topics/constitution.md).

### Transitional justice

*عدالت انتقالی* — The set of mechanisms a society uses after a period of repression or war to deal with past mass violations. In the UN framework it rests on four pillars: the right to truth, the right to justice, the right to reparations, and guarantees of non-recurrence. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

### Transitional National Security Council

*شورای امنیت ملی دوران گذار* — A body with a civilian majority that replaces the Supreme National Security Council during the transition. Military commanders sit as advisers without a majority, and a confidential committee of the national advisory assembly oversees it. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md), [Army](institutions/army.md).

### Transitional social pact

*پیمان اجتماعی گذار* — An agreement between the interim government, employer organizations and independent unions on the path of the minimum wage, wider insurance coverage, labor dispute resolution and freedom of association, inspired by Spain's Moncloa Pacts. See [Transition economy](topics/transition-economy.md).

### Transparent returned-assets fund

*صندوق شفاف دارایی‌های بازگشتی* — A single fund for all unfrozen or recovered assets, with an independent board of trustees and an international auditor, that spends only on predefined purposes such as medicine, food, economic stabilization and reparations, and publishes monthly reports. See [Sanctions and global finance](topics/sanctions-and-global-finance.md).

### Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT)

*پیمان منع گسترش سلاح‌های هسته‌ای (NPT)* — An international treaty that commits non-nuclear-weapon states not to build nuclear weapons and recognizes the right to peaceful nuclear energy. Iran signed it in 1968 and ratified it in 1970. See [Atomic energy](institutions/atomic-energy.md).

### Trustee management

*مدیریت امانی* — Placing the assets of an institution or company, by court order, under independent trustees (for example an auditor, a lawyer and an experienced manager) to preserve their value and prevent flight or looting. It is not confiscation; final decisions on the assets belong to elected bodies. See [Bonyads](institutions/bonyads.md), [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Truth commission

*کمیسیون حقیقت* — An independent body that builds the fullest possible picture of serious human rights violations, holds public hearings in the provinces and issues a public report with recommendations for reparations and reform. Its findings are not criminal verdicts; South Africa's Truth and Reconciliation Commission is the best-known example. See [Transitional justice](topics/transitional-justice.md).

## U

### Unarmed local committees

*کمیته‌های محلی بدون سلاح* — Neighborhood volunteer groups that, in the first days, work only unarmed and only to help and report, and hand over to official bodies by month six at the latest. See [Police](institutions/police.md), [Transition phases](transition-phases.md).

### Unified national army

*ارتش ملی یکپارچه* — A single armed force under civilian command into which the legitimate national-defense functions and conventional units of the IRGC are merged after individual vetting. The aim is that by the end of the transition there is only one national army. See [IRGC and Basij](institutions/irgc-basij.md), [Army](institutions/army.md).

## V

### Velayat-e faqih (guardianship of the Islamic jurist)

*ولایت فقیه* — A principle of the Islamic Republic's constitution which, under Article 5, entrusts leadership of the community during the Occultation to a just and pious jurist. The Leader's powers are set out in Article 110 and were expanded in the 1989 constitutional revision [citation needed]. See [Leadership and appointed councils](institutions/leadership-councils.md).

## W

### Whistleblower

*افشاگر* — Someone who reports corruption, abuse or law-breaking in their own institution through a safe channel. Under this plan, whistleblowers are legally protected from retaliation. See [Civil service](institutions/civil-service.md), [Anti-corruption](topics/anti-corruption.md).
