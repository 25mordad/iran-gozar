# How can I take part?

> **In plain words**
>
> This plan belongs to everyone and gets better with your criticism and corrections.
> You can just read it and pass it on, write your opinion, or fix the text yourself.
> To fix a text, click the "Fix this page" button at the bottom of any page.
> If you are in Iran, read the safe contribution guide first.

!!! warning "If you are in Iran, read this first"

    Taking part in this project may be risky for you. Before creating an account or writing anything,
    read the [guide to safe and pseudonymous contribution](project/security.md).
    Reading and sharing offline is the lowest-risk way to take part.

## Three ways, depending on your time

<div class="gozar-paths" markdown>

<div class="gozar-path" markdown>
<span class="gozar-path__time">1 minute</span>

### Read and share

- Read a document.
- Use the **Share** button at the bottom of the page to send it to others.
- Or **download the PDF** and share it offline.

Every new reader is a potential new critic.
</div>

<div class="gozar-path" markdown>
<span class="gozar-path__time">5 minutes</span>

### Give your opinion

- Click **"Critique this page"** at the bottom of any page.
- A simple form opens: write what you disagree with and why.
- You don't have to change the text yourself.

Your critique becomes a public "issue" on GitHub.
</div>

<div class="gozar-path" markdown>
<span class="gozar-path__time">15 minutes</span>

### Fix the text

- Click **"Fix this page"** at the bottom of any page.
- Correct the text, write a short explanation and submit.
- Volunteers will review it.

Step by step below.
</div>

</div>

## Fixing a page, step by step

You only need a free GitHub account. Nothing to install.

<div class="gozar-steps" markdown>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Click the fix button

At the bottom of any document on this site, click <span class="gh-btn gh-btn--green">:material-pencil: Fix this page</span>. It takes you to that document on GitHub.
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Sign in to GitHub

If you don't have an account, create one (**Sign up**). If you are in Iran, create an account that is not linked to your real identity: [safe contribution guide](project/security.md).
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Allow GitHub to make a copy

GitHub may say you need to make a copy to propose changes. Click <span class="gh-btn gh-btn--green">Fork this repository</span>. **Don't worry:** this is only your personal copy; the main text doesn't change until your proposal is reviewed.
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Correct the text

The document opens for editing. Fix whatever needs fixing. The <span class="gh-btn">Preview</span> tab shows what it will look like.

- If you add a source, also list it in the document's "Sources" section.
- If a claim has no source, mark it: `[citation needed]` (in Persian texts: `[نیاز به منبع]`).
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Save your change

Click the green <span class="gh-btn gh-btn--green">Commit changes…</span> button. In **Commit message**, say in one sentence what you did, e.g. "Fix typo in police document". Then click <span class="gh-btn gh-btn--green">Propose changes</span>.
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Send the request

The **Open a pull request** page opens with a ready-made form: write **what** you changed, **why**, and **where your source is**. Then click <span class="gh-btn gh-btn--green">Create pull request</span>.
</div>

<div class="gozar-step" markdown>
### Wait for review

Automated checks run, and a volunteer usually replies within about a week. They may ask a question or request a change — that means your text was read seriously.
</div>

</div>

The full guide, including the git workflow, writing rules and the pre-submission checklist: [contributing guide](project/contributing.md).

## How can you help?

<div class="gozar-cards" markdown>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### Find sources
Many claims in these documents still have no source. See the [list of claims awaiting a source](needs-sources.md) (the Persian list is much longer) and add a reliable source if you know one.
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### Share your experience
If you have worked in one of these institutions, or live in a province that is rarely heard, your knowledge is irreplaceable. [Write a critique](https://github.com/25mordad/iran-gozar/issues/new?template=critique.yml).
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### Bring your expertise
Lawyer, economist, doctor, water engineer, retired officer? See the [roles and skills needed](people-needed.md) and review the document in your field.
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### Translate
Most documents are only in Persian. Translate them into English, or into Azerbaijani Turkish, Kurdish, Arabic, Balochi or Iran's other languages. [Propose a translation](https://github.com/25mordad/iran-gozar/issues/new?template=translation.yml).
</div>

</div>

## No GitHub account?

- **Through an intermediary:** give your comment to someone you trust (preferably outside Iran) to post it in their own name.
- **Direct contact channels:** these are not set up yet; as soon as they are, their addresses will appear here.
- **In person:** read the PDF with people around you and talk about it.

Details in the [Without a GitHub account](project/contributing.md#without-a-github-account) section of the full guide.

!!! danger "Beware of fraud"

    This project accepts no donations, and no one will ever ask you in its name for your real name, phone number, address, password or money.
