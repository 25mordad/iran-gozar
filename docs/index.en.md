# Gozar

<p class="gozar-hero__tagline">An open plan for the day Iran crosses over to democracy: without collapse, without violence, without revenge</p>

<div class="gozar-what" markdown>

## What is Gozar?

*Gozar* means "crossing" or "transition" in Persian. If Iran's current political system changes one day, the country must stay standing: electricity, water, bread and medicine must keep flowing, salaries must be paid, no one should be killed in revenge, and people must choose their future through free elections. Gozar is a detailed map for exactly those days — from the first hours to a new constitution and free elections. It was drafted by an **artificial intelligence**, which says so openly. Everything is a draft, and **anyone can read it, criticize it and correct it.** The final decision always belongs to the people of Iran.

## Why pay attention to this plan?

<div class="gozar-cards gozar-cards--3" markdown>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
<span class="gozar-card__icon">:material-shield-home-outline:</span>

### The country keeps working

Civil servants, teachers, nurses, police and soldiers keep their jobs and salaries. No institution is dissolved overnight. [Transition phases](transition-phases.md)
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
<span class="gozar-card__icon">:material-scale-balance:</span>

### Justice, not revenge

Crimes are documented and tried in fair courts. Collective punishment, executions and purges are ruled out. [Manifesto](manifesto.md)
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
<span class="gozar-card__icon">:material-vote-outline:</span>

### The people decide

The form of government and the constitution are decided only by a free vote. The interim period has a hard deadline. [At a glance](at-a-glance.md)
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
<span class="gozar-card__icon">:material-robot-outline:</span>

### A transparent author

The draft's author is an AI that seeks no office, money or power and states its own limitations. [Manifesto](manifesto.md)
</div>
