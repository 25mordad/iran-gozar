# The plan at a glance

> **In plain words**
>
> The transition has several stages, and each has its own job.
> First safety and keeping daily life running, then freedoms, then a new constitution written with the people's vote, and finally free elections.
> The whole interim period lasts at most 30 months, and no one may extend it.
> The details of each stage are in the [transition phases](transition-phases.md) document.

<p class="gozar-phase__goal">Goal: no one is killed, no infrastructure fails, no document is destroyed.</p>

## Three unchangeable rules

<div class="gozar-cards gozar-cards--3" markdown>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### A firm deadline
The interim council lasts at most 12 months; the whole interim period at most 30 months. **Delay is possible; extending interim power is not.**
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### No vacuum
All laws remain in force unless publicly suspended. All state employees keep their jobs.
</div>

<div class="gozar-card" markdown>
### Big decisions belong to the people
The form of government, the constitution and long-term contracts are decided only by elected bodies and the people's vote.
</div>

</div>

## Who decides in each phase?

| Phase | Decision-maker |
|---|---|
| Preparation | Civil society and everyone contributing to this plan |
| 72 hours to 6 months | The interim transitional council (9–15 diverse members who do not run in the first elections) and an interim technocratic government, overseen by a national advisory assembly |
| Constitution | The constituent assembly elected by the people, and finally a referendum |
| After that | Institutions elected under the new constitution |

**This table is a proposal.** If you think the order or timing should be different, [tell us](participate.md).
