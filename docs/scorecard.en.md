# Transition scorecard

> **In plain words**
>
> Making promises is easy; what matters is checking them.
> This page lists what any transitional authority must do by a given deadline, and what it must never do.
> Print it, keep it, and if a transition ever begins, tick the boxes.
> If several red lines are crossed, that is a danger sign, and people should speak up.

## Why a scorecard?

Iran's history shows that the fervor of change can make promises fade: in 1979, many promises of freedom were forgotten within months. [citation needed] This scorecard lets people, media and observers judge **by specific criteria** instead of general impressions. It belongs to no group or person: **anyone** who takes responsibility for a transition is measured against it — including those who use this very plan. The criteria come from the [transition phases](transition-phases.md) and the red lines of the [manifesto](manifesto.md).

## Red lines: at any time

Check these on every day of the transition. **Even one violation is a serious warning.**

| ✔ | Criterion | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | No execution has been carried out. | Human rights organizations and families |
| ☐ | No one has been detained without a judicial order, and all detainees have access to a lawyer. | Bar associations and families |
| ☐ | There are no credible reports of torture or forced confessions. | Independent monitors, prison visits |
| ☐ | No group has been punished for membership in an institution, ethnicity, religion or family. | Tracking collective dismissals and arrests |
| ☐ | No armed force has been dissolved overnight without a plan. | Published decisions of the interim council |
| ☐ | No journalist or critic has been jailed or threatened for criticizing the interim authority. | Journalists' associations |
| ☐ | No foreign army has been invited to carry out the transition. | Official documents and statements |
| ☐ | No official decision discriminates by religion, ethnicity, language, gender or belief. | Decisions and civil-society reports |
| ☐ | No unelected body has extended the transition timetable. | The official calendar against this scorecard |

## First 72 hours

| ✔ | Criterion |
|---|---|
| ☐ | A "no revenge" message and an order for armed forces to return to barracks have been publicly announced. |
| ☐ | The interim transitional council's members, commitments and deadline have been publicly announced. |
| ☐ | The council's first resolution is published: executions and corporal punishments halted, unelected councils' powers suspended, offices keep working. |
| ☐ | It has been officially announced that all salaries, pensions and subsidies will be paid. |
| ☐ | Electricity, water, bread, fuel and medicine are available in every province. |
| ☐ | There is one official information channel with a daily briefing. |
| ☐ | Prisons have not been thrown open, but release of political prisoners on verified lists has begun. |
| ☐ | No major archive (security agencies, prisons, financial records) has been destroyed or looted. |
| ☐ | Nuclear facilities and weapons depots are secure. |
| ☐ | Mosques, churches, synagogues, other places of worship and clerics have been protected. |

## First 30 days

| ✔ | Criterion |
|---|---|
| ☐ | The interim technocratic government is complete and all ministries are working. |
| ☐ | Salaries and pensions have been paid on time at least once. |
| ☐ | All known political prisoners and prisoners of conscience have been released. |
| ☐ | Compulsory dress rules have ended and the "morality police" have been dissolved. |
| ☐ | The internet is available without filtering. |
| ☐ | The press is free and owning a satellite dish is no longer a crime. |
| ☐ | The exchange rate and basic-goods prices are no longer in free fall. |
| ☐ | An evidence and archives preservation board exists and people can file complaints. |
| ☐ | A draft transitional charter has been published for public consultation. |
| ☐ | The national advisory assembly exists and meets in public. |

## Month six

| ✔ | Criterion |
|---|---|
| ☐ | The transitional charter has been adopted and published. |
| ☐ | The independent electoral commission exists and the constituent assembly election date is announced. |
| ☐ | A transitional justice law has been adopted and the truth commission has started work. |
| ☐ | The transition budget has been published. |
| ☐ | No armed group outside state command controls any city. |
| ☐ | A plan for reforming the armed forces (including integration and disarmament) has been published. |
| ☐ | Every decision of the interim council has been published within 24 hours. |

## Up to the constitution and elections

| ✔ | Criterion | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Free constituent assembly elections with paper ballots and public counting. | Month 10 at the latest |
| ☐ | The interim council dissolved at the constituent assembly's first session. | Month 12 at the latest |
| ☐ | No interim council member ran in the constituent assembly election. | That election |
| ☐ | The assembly's drafts and minutes are public, with public hearings in every province. | During drafting |
| ☐ | At least nine months of free, balanced debate before any referendum on the form of government. | Before that referendum |
| ☐ | The constitution adopted in a free referendum with domestic and international observers. | About month 24 |
| ☐ | General elections held under the new constitution. | Month 30 at the latest |
| ☐ | Power formally and publicly handed to elected institutions. | Month 30 at the latest |

## How to use this scorecard

- **Print it:** the "Download PDF" button at the bottom of this page gives a printable version.
- **Publish it:** media and observers can fill it in and publish it every month.
- **Improve it:** if an important criterion is missing, [propose it](https://github.com/25mordad/iran-gozar/issues/new?template=critique.yml&document=docs/scorecard.md).
