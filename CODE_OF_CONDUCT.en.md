# Contributor Code of Conduct

[نسخه‌ی فارسی](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

> **In plain words**
>
> Everyone here treats each other with respect, even in strong disagreement.
> Attack ideas, not people; insults, threats and labels like "traitor" or "mercenary" are not allowed.
> Never reveal the real identity or location of someone who writes under a pseudonym, because it could put their life at risk.
> If you see bad behavior, tell the maintainers so they can act.

## Why this code matters

Gozar is about the future of a country whose people have been wounded for years and who disagree deeply. If we cannot argue respectfully in an open-source repository, we cannot claim to have a plan for a non-violent transition. This code is a small exercise in the very culture we hope to see in a future Iran.

Many contributors may live in Iran, and contributing may carry real risk for them. **Their safety comes before everything else.**

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project a safe and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of ethnicity, language, religion or lack of it, gender, gender identity and sexual orientation, age, disability, appearance, class, education, place of residence (inside or outside Iran), nationality or political opinion.

## Expected behavior

- **Criticize ideas, not people.** "This argument has no source" is fine; "you are ignorant" is not.
- **Good faith.** Assume others also want the best for Iran unless proven otherwise.
- **Accuracy.** Support claims with sources, and admit mistakes.
- **Listen to lived experience.** The experience of someone who has lived in Sistan-Baluchestan, Kurdistan, Khuzestan or anywhere else is valid, even if it differs from your picture of Iran.
- **Patience with newcomers.** Many people are using GitHub for the first time. Guide them; don't mock them.
- **Respect for languages.** Contributions in Persian, English or any other language of Iran are welcome. If someone's Persian or English is imperfect, focus on the substance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- **Doxxing:** revealing, or trying to uncover, the real identity, location, workplace, contact details or family of any pseudonymous contributor. This is the most serious violation in this project and leads to a permanent ban.
- **Pressure to disclose:** repeatedly asking someone "where do you live?", "who are you really?" or "are you inside or outside Iran?".
- **Insults and degradation** based on ethnicity, language, accent, religion, gender, orientation, appearance or any other characteristic.
- **Political labeling:** calling opponents "traitor", "mercenary", "separatist", "monarchist" or "infiltrator" instead of answering their arguments.
- **Threats or incitement to violence** against any person or group, including officials or supporters of the current system.
- **Harassment:** persistent following, unwanted messages, unwanted sexual content.
- **Deliberate misinformation** or fabricated sources.
- **Spam and advertising** for a group, party, person or product.
- **Fundraising** in the name of this project (the project currently accepts no donations).

## Strong disagreement is allowed

This project deals with issues on which people disagree profoundly: the form of government, federalism, the role of religion, transitional justice. **Strong, blunt, even heated disagreement with a text or proposal is entirely allowed.** Attacking people is not. For how content disputes are resolved, see [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Reporting

If you see behavior that violates this code:

1. **If someone's safety is at risk** (for example their identity has been exposed), tell the maintainers immediately. Maintainers can hide or delete the content.
2. For other violations, open an issue with the "رفتار" (conduct) label or, if you prefer privacy, use the private channels described in the [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.en.md#without-a-github-account).
3. The reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report and respond in proportion to its severity:

| Level | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | A one-off demeaning tone | Private or public note and a request to correct |
| 2. Warning | Repeated insults or labeling | Formal warning; messages may be hidden |
| 3. Temporary ban | Sustained harassment | Temporary ban from participation |
| 4. Permanent ban | Doxxing, threats of violence | Permanent removal from the project |

Every enforcement decision is recorded with its reasons, and the person may request one review.
